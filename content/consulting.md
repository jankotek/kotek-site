<ul>

<li>Performance optimization and redesign</li>
<li>Code audit and review</li>
<li>Support for MapDB</li>
<li>New features and bug-fixing</li>
<li>Refactoring and migration of existing code</li>
</ul>



<h2>MapDB support</h2>

MapDB is an open-source project and basic support is provided for free. 
However, my time is limited and free support might be slow. 
I usually respond to queries within a few days. Less urgent bug-fixes might take weeks,
and new features might take months (or years). 



I do not answer more specific questions about performance and data design for free. 
And finally free support is not confidential (with exceptions), as
I might ask for testimonial, or forward  answers to the mailing list. 



More serious users might prefer paid services. 
That includes:
<ul>
<li>Faster response times
<li>Meetings over the phone or video calls
<li>Priority bug-fixing
<li>New features tailored to your use case
<li>Code review for possible performance optimizations, concurrency issues, lower memory usage... 
<li>Confidentiality
<li>Best solution recommendation (in many cases MapDB can be combined with other libraries)
<li>Training
</ul>
